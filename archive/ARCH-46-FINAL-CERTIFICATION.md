## 1. What was built

**Every date a document commits you to, owned by a person, on a calendar, and told to Flow Builder exactly once.** Renewals, notice deadlines, payments, deliveries, expiries and reports are read from processed documents (and added by hand); each has an owner, a state (OPEN / DUE_SOON / OVERDUE / DONE / WAIVED), the evidence it was read from and the arithmetic that produced its date.

| Kind | Read from | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| RENEWAL | an auto-renewing term ("renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months") | a series: start + term, then every renewal period (`FREQ=YEARLY;INTERVAL=n` or monthly), joined at the first occurrence on or after the day it is read |
| NOTICE | "not less than sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term" | **renewal − notice period** (the day before the renewal when it says "end of the term"), rolled PRECEDING to a business day; counts from the renewal obligation and moves with it |
| EXPIRY | a fixed term, "valid until", a Period of Insurance line | start + term − 1 day, or the stated date |
| PAYMENT | "payable on the 5th day of each month", "annually on 1 April", an invoice's due date or date + "Net 30" | a series, a stated date, or an offset from the invoice date |
| DELIVERY | "deliver the Data Inventory by 30 April 2027", "within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date" | a stated date or an offset from the effective date |
| REPORTING | "within ten (10) business days after the end of each calendar quarter" | a series of period ends + the offset (business days on the workspace's holiday calendar) |

**Deterministic, model-free extraction.** ARCH-45's clause segmentation (running headers and footers removed, a clause across a page break kept whole) and typed values ("forty-five (45) days" is one duration; dates DMY/MDY with the document's own evidence and the workspace's date format as the tie-break), ARCH-33's compiled notice-period reader as an independent cross-check (a disagreement lowers confidence and says so), and the temporal rules below. Nothing calls a model; nothing is billed. Each draft carries a source key (sha256 of kind, rule, anchor and signature): re-reading a document keeps what people did (owner, state, review, edits), revives what the document says again and SUPERSEDES what it no longer says. An obligation read with a doubt — an ambiguous 04/05/2026, business days counted without a holiday calendar, an ARCH-33 disagreement, a date the document did not give — is PENDING: an eighth review-hub kind (`OBLIGATION`, reason `OBLIGATION_UNCONFIRMED`), and it reaches Flow Builder only once a person confirms it.

**The temporal rules** (stated once in `obligations/temporal.py`, each gated): (1) months and years keep the day, else the month's last day — 31 Jan + 1 month = 28 Feb (29 Feb in 2028), 29 Feb 2028 + 1 year = 28 Feb 2029, + 4 years = 29 Feb 2032; (2) series are anchored on their start, never chained (31 Jan, 28 Feb, 31 Mar, 30 Apr — not 28 Apr); (3) business days skip the calendar's weekend days and STORED holidays, the start day never counted; (4) a notice deadline is anchor − period; (5) roll conventions NONE / FOLLOWING / PRECEDING / MODIFIED_FOLLOWING, notices PRECEDING; (6) a due date is a LOCAL date in the workspace's zone — OVERDUE from the local midnight after it, DUE_SOON from `lead_days` before it; (7) RRULE (RFC 5545, DAILY/WEEKLY/MONTHLY/YEARLY, nothing below a day): plain monthly/yearly rules follow rules 1–2, BY* rules follow RFC 5545 exactly (python-dateutil), where a day that does not exist is skipped.

**Holiday calendars** are rows (`holiday_calendars`: a typed `date[]` with names, CHECKed strictly ascending, weekend days as ISO weekdays, one default per workspace). They are filled from built-in templates — US federal (5 U.S.C. 6103 with OPM observance, including New Year's Day observed on the previous 31 December), England & Wales (Easter by the Gregorian computus, substitute days), India national, weekends only — pasted dates, or an .ics file (yearly RRULEs expanded). No calendar API is called. Editing a calendar reschedules every obligation that counts with it.

**The sweep** (`scripts/sweep_obligations.py`, `flowpilot-sweep obligations`, scheduled HOURLY in `deploy/cron.d/flowpilot-sweepers`) runs in UTC and evaluates every workspace at its own local date, so each zone's midnight is caught within the hour. Entering DUE_SOON or OVERDUE inserts an `obligation_events` row that a partial UNIQUE index allows once per (obligation, state, due date); only when that insert happens is `trigger.obligation.due_soon` / `trigger.obligation.overdue` emitted, with the idempotency key `<event>:<obligation>:<due date>` (the outbox refuses a duplicate too). Run it twice, or every hour: the second run changes and emits nothing; a due date moved later alerts again (new key); a state forced back is restored silently. It also links extracted obligations to ARCH-42 parties resolved after reading. Organizations without the plan are skipped.

**Calendar feeds.** A member issues a feed (all obligations, or only theirs); the URL carries `v1.<32 random bytes>.<HMAC-SHA256 under a key HKDF-derived from the configured encryption keys, 16 bytes>`, shown once. Only its SHA-256 is stored (`char(64)`, hex CHECK, UNIQUE). The public route (`/api/v1/public/calendar-feeds/{token}.ics`, in `PUBLIC_ROUTES`) checks the signature in constant time under every configured key (rotation-safe, no early exit) before the database is asked, then the hash, revocation, expiry, the member's membership, the user's account and the plan — and answers every refusal with the IDENTICAL `404 Not found.` (same bytes, same headers, `Cache-Control: no-store`), so revocation takes effect on the calendar app's next fetch. The feed is RFC 5545: all-day events (a due date is a date, not an instant), CRLF, folded at 75 octets without splitting UTF-8, reminders at the obligation's lead time, series written occurrence by occurrence (each rolled on its own). Tokens in public paths are now redacted from logs: the request log, uvicorn's access log (a filter on the console handler) and the Caddy access log (`format filter` on `request>uri`) — ARCH-43's document-request tokens gain the same protection.

**Data.** `obligations` (composite FKs onto `workspaces`, `work_items` (CASCADE: what was read from a document goes with it), `entities` (`SET NULL (entity_id)`), itself (the anchor; a trigger refuses cycles and chains deeper than four), `holiday_calendars` and `workspace_members` (the owner must be a member; losing access clears it); CHECKs tie every state to its timestamps, the rule kind to the recurrence, extracted rows to their source key; an evidence trigger refuses a span on another document), `obligation_events` (every transition; the once-only alert index), `calendar_feed_tokens` (the member FK cascades: losing access loses the feeds), `holiday_calendars`. A `work_items` trigger unlinks a person's own obligations before a document is deleted. Zero column drift between models and migration.

**Wiring.** Job `obligations.extract_document` on the LIGHT profile, enqueued by post-enrichment 90 s after the document (so ARCH-42 has resolved its parties) and by "Read again"; two Flow Builder triggers (21 triggers over 22 events; live conformance ALL CONFORMANT); the hub kind; ARCH-20 erasure deletes extracted obligations (they quote the document) and unlinks a person's own; Entity 360's obligations (the ARCH-42 placeholder) now filled, following `merged_into_id` so a merge never orphans them.

**API** (22 routes, each gated on the capability as its second line; reads VIEWER, changes CONTRIBUTOR, holiday calendars ADMIN, a member's own feeds): list (filters, counts, the workspace's today and zone), create, calendar (projected occurrences), date calculator, export (.ics / .csv, audited, injection-safe), detail (derivation, evidence, history, upcoming occurrences, anchor and dependents), edit (a new date, rule, owner, lead time, calendar — dependents follow), complete (a series advances to its next occurrence), waive (a reason), reopen, review, delete (a person's own only), a document's obligations, read again, a record's obligations, holiday calendars (list with templates, create, edit, delete), feeds (list, issue, revoke).

**Console.** *Obligations* in the workspace navigation (capability-locked; the plan card lists it): a list (state chips with counts, kind, mine, search, .ics/.csv), a month calendar built from YYYY-MM-DD strings (no browser time-zone shift; later occurrences of a series marked as projected), holiday calendars (template / pasted / .ics, default, dates), calendar feeds (the URL shown once, copy, webcal link, revoke), "New obligation" (a date, an offset in days/business days/weeks/months/years, or a repeating rule, with "Check the date" showing every arithmetic step before saving), and the obligation page (actions, derivation, evidence, history marking what Flow Builder was told). An *Obligations* tab on every document's details (with "Read again") and an *Obligations* section on every Entity 360 record; the review hub's *Obligations* tab with Confirm all / Reject all.

## 8. Found and fixed during the build

- **The service's own `update()` shadowed SQLAlchemy's** (`from sqlalchemy import update` then `def update(...)`): every bulk update in the module — feed use counts, the default calendar switch, erasure's unlinking, deleting an anchor — called the person's-edit function. Found by D5/D7/D8/D12; SQLAlchemy's is imported as `sa_update`.
- A rule posted without `sign` or `shift_days` (JSON `null`) failed with `int(None)`: nulls now take the defaults (found by D5).
- Deleting an anchor reset a WAIVED dependent to OPEN while keeping its waiver timestamp (a CHECK violation): closed dependents stay closed.
- **Feed tokens reached the logs.** The request log, uvicorn's access log and the Caddy access log all wrote the full path — so did ARCH-43's document-request tokens. Now redacted in all three (L1, L2, D7).
- **The Caddyfile no longer started on current Caddy.** `caddy:2-alpine` is 2.10, which refuses `on_demand_tls { interval … burst … }` — a pre-existing deployment blocker found while validating the log filter with a real Caddy; both options are removed (the ask endpoint is the control).
- **A person's own obligation was deleted with a linked document** (the composite FK cascades): a `work_items` trigger now unlinks them first (D3, MD21).
- **ARCH-45's segmenter split a clause whose last line has no ascenders** ("payable on 31 December of each / year." — the lower glyph box read as a paragraph gap), found by a 1,000-document stress run: a line that starts lower-case after a line that does not end a sentence now always continues it. `verify_arch45` passes unchanged; G9 and MS44 pin it.
- The API read the wall clock directly while the service read `service.now()`; one clock now (the gates pin it).
- Pre-existing, fixed because ARCH-46 touched the same surface: `scripts/verify_arch30_tranche3.py` **H8** failed on raw `new Date(…).toLocale*String()` in four ARCH-42/43 pages (Entity 360, Entities, CaseDetail, the public document-request page); they now use the profile-aware formatters, and calendar dates use the new `formatCalendarDate` / `formatCalendarMonth` (profile language, never zone-shifted). H8 now passes (8/8).

## 9. Corrections to the prompt and roadmap

- **"python-dateutil is installed only transitively — pin it."** It is already pinned (`python-dateutil==2.9.0.post0`, and `tzdata==2026.2` for Windows) in `requirements.txt` (a UTF-16LE file). Nothing to add.
- **"A nightly sweep."** A single nightly run in UTC would catch each zone's midnight at a different local hour, up to a day late. The sweep is scheduled hourly and evaluates each workspace at its own local date; it is idempotent, so each workspace effectively has a nightly sweep at its own midnight.
- **"Exactly once per obligation per state."** Once per obligation, state AND due date: a renewal that moves, or a recurring payment's next occurrence, must alert again. The key and the unique index carry the due date.
- **"An Obligations tab on Entity 360."** Entity 360 has no tab bar; its obligations are a section (the ARCH-42 placeholder, now filled). Work Item details does have tabs; the Obligations tab is there.
- The prompt listed the review-hub kinds; ARCH-46 adds an eighth (`OBLIGATION`), because an obligation read with a doubt must not reach Flow Builder unconfirmed.
- `scripts/verify_arch21.py` **G8** flags every `/api/v1/public/*` route registered public (pre-existing since ARCH-43's document requests); the feed follows ARCH-43's pattern as the prompt asked, so G8 lists it too.
